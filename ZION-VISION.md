# Zion A.D. — Design direction

**One game. One codebase. The complete 0 A.D. foundation.**

The player's goal is to cultivate Zion over a 2,000-year span. A society is
more than armies and GDP; culture, interior experience, individual behavior,
and institutions interact. An aspirational integral framework supplies four
perspectives (individual interior and exterior; collective interior and exterior).

## Native-game priorities

1. Update native game identity and title-screen presentation to Zion A.D.
2. Extend 0 A.D.'s simulation components with persistent societal metrics:
   provision, material prosperity, dignity, freedom, wisdom, communion,
   ecological health, resilience, and intergenerational memory.
3. Make governance a real, changeable institution inside the RTS UI:
   community covenant, elder council, monarchy, constitutional charter,
   mercantile republic, assemblies, or federated commons.
4. Connect player choices (laws, welfare, trade, resource allocation, civic
   participation, diplomacy, military action) to observable outcomes.
5. Implement era transitions spanning 2,000 years, including time-dependent
   technology, urbanization, and societal challenges.
6. Provide native-game scenarios and a Zion victory condition rewarding
   plural, sustainable flourishing rather than expansion or domination.
7. Add dedicated art and audio only after the original game renders and
   performs reliably.

## Guardrails

* Spiral-development stages are optional game heuristics, not a scientific
  measure of persons or cultures. Development is contingent and reversible.
* Governance structures have benefits, costs, and failure modes; no
  institution or strategy is guaranteed success.
* No forced conversion, ethnic hierarchy, or persecution is ever rewarded.
* Preserve upstream source, art provenance, and license notices.
* No new browser simulator; edit the real C++/JavaScript/Pyrogenesis code.
